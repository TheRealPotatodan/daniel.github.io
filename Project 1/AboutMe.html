Why do I enjoy Splatoon?

Splatoon has been my best friend since 2019 ever since I got my first Switch. Though its a very hard game, and often makes me want to pull my hair out, its story and gameplay have always been rich and engaging! I highly reccomend this game to others, but the motion controlls can be a bit tricky to get used to in the beginning. (Not even I was the exception > .>) ​
